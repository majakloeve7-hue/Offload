Welcome to Offload.

Life can get overwhelming. Your brain shouldn't have to carry all that heavy weight alone. Offload gives you a quiet place to write things out, then move into a short seasonal activity designed to help you slow down and reset.

Privacy in this prototype: what you type stays only in this open browser session. It is not uploaded or saved, and refreshing or closing the page clears it.
